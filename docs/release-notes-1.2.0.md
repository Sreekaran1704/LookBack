LookBack 1.2.0: say it out loud. Voice memories, photos that look like old polaroids, a fluffier bunny, and everything feels smoother.

## What's new

- **Voice memories.** Tap Voice at the bottom of the writing page and talk (up to a minute); the waveform draws itself as you speak. A voice memory is a polaroid: on its own, the waveform is the picture; with a photo, the photo sits on top and the waveform runs along the strip underneath. Tap it to listen: the bars fill in as it plays and a little heart rides along. Removing a voice note asks first.
- **Photos look like old instant film.** A touch of sepia, faded colours, a soft vignette and a glossy sheen, on every polaroid and taped photo.
- **A fluffier, livelier bunny.** Bun now has soft fur and a little curl on its head. It blinks (sometimes twice), breathes, tilts its head now and then, and squishes and hops when you tap it.
- **Buttons where your thumb is.** Every page's buttons sit in one taped paper bar at the bottom, each with a label: Back · Listen · Turn over · Delete on a memory; Close · Photo · Style · Voice · Update when writing. Photo and Style open their choices right above the bar, so nothing hides below the fold.
- **The bar steps aside.** Home / Years / Settings only shows on those three pages; inside a memory or a diary you just see that page's own buttons.
- **Feels nicer in the hand.** Buttons click down and up, sheets glide in and out, and there are gentle haptics for opening, landing, toggling and saving, using Android's own crisp haptic effects.

## Smoother

- Opening a memory from Home: the slowest frame went from about 1 second to about 20 ms during the slide.
- Opening a diary from the shelf: the slowest frame went from 150 ms to 44 ms (the book is now built in stages instead of all at once).

## Fixed

- The words "On this day" and the date now sit on the same line, and the bunny beside each page title is as tall as the title.
- The delete dialog no longer bounces in.
- The "display over other apps" permission (left over from development) is gone.

## Install

Download `LookBack-1.2.0.apk` below on your Android phone and open it (allow installing unknown apps if asked). It installs over 1.1.0 and keeps your memories. Needs Android 7+ on a 64-bit (arm64) phone.
