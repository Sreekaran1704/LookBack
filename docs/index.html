LookBack

one small thing a day · meet it again next year

LookBack is a leather-bound diary for one small thing a day. Write a few words, tuck in a photo, or record your voice. On the very same day next year, it comes back to you as a letter from your past self.

What's inside

  • Letters from your past self: last year's page arrives sealed, on the same date.
  • Write back: answer your past self, in sepia ink under the original.
  • Voice on tape: up to a minute, played on a little leather tape recorder.
  • A diary for every year, standing on a bookshelf.
  • Give a page to someone you love: straight from your phone to theirs, encrypted.

Private by design

Your diary stays on your phone. No ads, no tracking. Backup, if you want it, goes to a “LookBack” folder in your own Google Drive, and pages you give never touch a server.
