LookBack privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026 · applies to the LookBack app for Android (package com.yearbook.app)

LookBack is a little journal: you save one small thing a day (a few words, a photo, or a voice note) and the app brings it back on the same day next year. It is made by an individual developer. This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your memories stay on your phone, and in your own Google Drive only if you switch backup on. We have no servers, no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

What stays on your phone

  • Your memories: the words you write, the date, the sticker and tape you choose.
  • Photos you add: a copy of the photo you pick or take, shrunk and kept inside the app's own storage.
  • Voice notes you record: the recording and a small drawing of its waveform, kept inside the app's own storage.
  • Your settings: the name you give the app, your paper and colours, your reminder time, and (if you use backup) the email address of the Google account you chose and when the last backup ran.

None of this is sent to the developer or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it from your phone. Android's own automatic backup is switched off for LookBack.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Drive, LookBack copies your memories (words, photos and voice notes) into a folder called “LookBack” in your own Google Drive, and can restore them from there, for example on a new phone.

  • The app asks only for Google's drive.file permission: it can see and change only the files it created itself, not anything else in your Drive.
  • The files go straight from your phone to Google over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). The developer never receives them and cannot see them.
  • Your use of Google Drive is covered by Google's privacy policy.
  • You can disconnect at any time in Settings → Backup, and delete the “LookBack” folder from your Drive whenever you like.

LookBack's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Microphone: only to record a voice note, and only while you are recording one.
  • Camera and photos: only when you choose to take or pick a photo for a memory.
  • Notifications: to remind you, on the day, of memories from earlier years. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; no server is involved.
  • Internet: only for Google sign-in and Drive backup.

Deleting your data

  • Delete any memory in the app (its photo and voice note go with it). With backup on, it is also removed from your Drive at the next backup.
  • Uninstall the app to remove everything from your phone.
  • Delete the “LookBack” folder in Google Drive to remove your backup.

Children

LookBack is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. It collects nothing for the developer from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions? Open an issue at github.com/Sreekaran1704/LookBack/issues.
