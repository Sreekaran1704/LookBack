LookBack 1.1.0: you can now let a memory go, Bun shreds it, and Google Drive stops asking you to connect again.

## What's new

- **Delete a memory.** Every memory's page has a Delete button. Bun asks first on a little paper card ("Let this one go?"), then plays a tiny scene: its head opens like a lid, the memory floats out of its brain and drops into a paper shredder with a face. The memory is gone from your phone, and from your Drive backup at the next backup, so it never comes back from another copy.
- **The bottom bar matches your paper.** It used to be the same cream strip on every page. Now it's made from the paper you picked: a brighter sheet of it in light, a lamp-lit shade of it in dark, with tape in that paper's own colours. All 7 papers × light/dark.

## Fixed

- **Google Drive kept asking to connect.** About once a day, the quiet automatic backup opened Google's account picker, so it looked as if Drive had to be connected again. It now uses the sign-in your phone already has and never shows a sheet on its own. If Google ever signs your phone out, "Back up now" asks once.

## Install

Download `LookBack-1.1.0.apk` below on your Android phone and open it (allow installing unknown apps if asked). It installs over 1.0.0 and keeps your memories. Needs Android 7+ on a 64-bit (arm64) phone.
